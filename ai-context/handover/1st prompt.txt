Ensure the handover documentation explains how to:

## 8. Update documentation

Update the existing handover documents with verified findings.

Keep sensitive information out of the public repository, including credentials, private infrastructure details and operational recovery secrets.

If information is sensitive, report it privately rather than committing it.

## Safety restrictions

- Do not change production settings or data.
- Do not modify permissions without approval.
- Do not deploy, merge, push or trigger synchronisation jobs.
- Do not execute database migrations.
- Do not expose credentials or secrets.
- Do not make unrelated application changes.
- Do not run lint, tests, typecheck or build commands.
- Do not claim a backup or rollback is verified unless its recovery procedure has actually been demonstrated.
- If an external system cannot be accessed, report it as unverified rather than assuming success.
- Ask for approval before any action that could affect the live application.

5. **Risks:** Anything that could prevent safe maintenance.

Finish with one of these conclusions:

- READY
- READY WITH RESTRICTIONS
