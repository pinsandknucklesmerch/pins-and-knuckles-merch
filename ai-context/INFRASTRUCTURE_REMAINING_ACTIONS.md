## Recovery boundaries and incident steps

Application rollback and database restoration are different operations. A Vercel rollback does not recover deleted records, reverse migrations or restore external configuration. The existing database restore script is destructive and restricted to disposable local/test targets; it intentionally excludes sessions, refresh tokens, MFA, Storage objects and other platform state. Never remove its safeguards to target production.

1. Salesperson stops retries, records the affected page, time and business impact, and contacts management and technical support through the private channel.
2. Technical support determines whether the failure concerns application code, an external service, account access or data loss. Preserve evidence and confirm effects on both Hub and Merch.
3. Management approves the specific intervention, expected downtime and any data loss. If a recovery source or authority is missing, escalate to provider support; do not improvise a restore.
4. Technical support follows the verified provider-supported recovery procedure, validates business workflows and monitors the result. Close the incident only after agreed acceptance.
